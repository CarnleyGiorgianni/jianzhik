1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how Jianzhik ("we", "us", "our" or "the Studio"), an independent product studio based at Arizona State University Sky Song Innovation Campus, Tempe, Arizona, United States, collects, uses, discloses, and protects information in connection with:

  • The website jianzhik.com and all subdomains (collectively, the "Website");
  • All mobile, tablet, desktop and visionOS applications published by Jianzhik on the Apple App Store, Google Play, Huawei AppGallery, Xiaomi GetApps, Samsung Galaxy Store, Microsoft Store, Amazon Appstore, Mac App Store and any other distribution platform we may adopt from time to time (collectively, the "Apps");
  • Any related services, APIs, marketing, support or communications (collectively with the Website and the Apps, the "Services").

By installing, accessing or otherwise using the Services, you confirm that you have read, understood and agreed to this Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use. We will not knowingly collect personal data in a manner inconsistent with this Policy.

2. Data Controller

The data controller for the Services is Jianzhik, located at Arizona State University Sky Song Innovation Campus, Tempe, AZ, United States. For GDPR purposes, our representative within the Union can be reached at contact@jianzhik.com. For UK GDPR purposes our representative is reachable at the same address. For PIPL purposes our local representative is reachable at contact@jianzhik.com.

3. Definitions

  • "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • "Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Data.
  • "End-User" means any individual who installs or uses our Apps.
  • "Device Data" means information automatically collected from the device on which the App is installed (model, OS version, locale, IP address, advertising identifiers).
  • "Advertising Identifier" means Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) or Google's Advertising ID (GAID) depending on platform.
  • "SDK" means a third-party software development kit integrated for a specific purpose such as advertising, attribution or analytics.
  • "Sensitive Data" means special category data such as health, biometric, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, trade union membership, genetic or biometric data for uniquely identifying a person, sex life or sexual orientation.

4. Data We Collect

4.1 Data you provide voluntarily

  • Email address, name and message contents when you contact us via the Website form or via email.
  • Email address if you subscribe to our field-notes newsletter (handled by a third-party privacy-respecting email service).
  • Account information you create inside an App, such as locally-stored credentials (we do not maintain a central account database by design).
  • Support correspondence, including bug reports, screenshots and device metadata you voluntarily share.

4.2 Data collected automatically by the Apps

  • Device model, OS version, locale, time zone, screen resolution, free storage, network connectivity type.
  • App version, build number, language, region settings, in-app feature usage counts (with your opt-in).
  • Advertising Identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android) — collected only after explicit opt-in via the platform's tracking prompt.
  • Crash logs, performance counters (CPU, memory, frame rate), error stack traces — stored locally and only uploaded if you opt in to share diagnostics.
  • Frequency-cap and consent signals managed by the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) or equivalent SDK.

4.3 Data collected by the Website

  • Server logs (IP address, user agent, referer, URL, response code) retained for 30 days for security purposes only.
  • First-party cookies that remember your consent and theme preferences. We do not set advertising cookies on this Website.
  • Aggregated analytics from a privacy-respecting self-hosted analytics tool (no cross-site profiling, no IP storage, no fingerprinting).

4.4 Data we never collect

  • Government identification numbers, biometric identifiers, race, ethnicity, religion, political affiliation, sexual orientation.
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, location, Bluetooth, NFC, HealthKit, HomeKit, Calendar, Reminders data — unless explicitly required for a documented in-app feature that you opt into.
  • Personal Data of children under the age of 13 anywhere in the world, or under the higher age defined by local law where applicable.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the GDPR, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for advertising personalisation, analytics, newsletter subscriptions, diagnostic uploads.
  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — to deliver the service you have installed or subscribed to.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — for security, fraud prevention, aggregated product analytics.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — for compliance with tax, accounting and law enforcement requests.

Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.

6. How We Use Personal Data

  • To provide, operate, maintain, secure and improve the Services.
  • To deliver core in-app functionality that runs entirely on your device.
  • To display advertising and measure its performance (only after explicit consent on iOS 14.5+ / iPadOS 14.5+ / tvOS 14.5+ and equivalent Android versions).
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide support.
  • To send the field-notes newsletter (only with your explicit subscription).
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • To comply with our legal obligations, enforce our terms, and protect our rights and the rights of others.

7. Advertising & Monetisation Overview

AdMob at the heart: Our free Apps integrate Google AdMob for monetisation. AdMob may collect Device Data, Advertising Identifier, coarse location (where permitted), and interaction data to select and serve ads. AdMob and its mediation vendors process data under Google's Ads Data Processing Terms and the EU/Swiss/UK GDPR-compliant contractual framework.

All ad-serving activity is gated by the platform's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt on Apple platforms and the equivalent Play Console prompts on Android. Until you grant consent, we serve contextual ads only and pass no IDFA/GAID to ad networks. You may withdraw consent at any time via the in-app "Privacy Choices" panel which is surfaced in every App's settings.

8. Google AdMob Specific Disclosures

We use Google AdMob (and where applicable, AdMob Mediation). AdMob may collect and process:

  • IP address, device identifiers including IDFA (iOS) / GAID (Android), cookie identifiers, Advertising IDs.
  • App metadata, ad impressions, clicks, conversions.
  • Coarse location derived from IP (no precise GPS).
  • Information about the device, OS version, language, carrier, time zone.

AdMob uses this data to select, serve and measure ads. You can opt out of personalised ads via:

  • Apple: Settings > Tracking > "Allow Apps to Request to Track" > toggle off for Jianzhik Apps, or via our in-app "Privacy Choices" panel.
  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > "Opt out of Ads Personalisation".
  • Web: adssettings.google.com.

AdMob and Google are signatories to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, the UK Extension, and the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework where applicable, providing adequate safeguards for transfers from the EEA, Switzerland and UK to the United States. Google's EU and UK GDPR-compliant Data Processing Terms apply. For more information, please consult the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Advertising Policies.

12. Tracking, IDFA & Privacy Choices

  • On iOS / iPadOS / tvOS 14.5+ we use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. The prompt reads (verbatim): "Allow [App Name] to track you across other companies' apps and websites?".
  • Until you tap "Allow", we will not pass IDFA to any third party. Ads will be contextual only.
  • On Android 12+ we honour the Advertising ID and the "Opt out of Ads personalization" toggle.
  • You can revisit your choice at any time via the in-app "Privacy Choices" panel or via the platform settings listed in section 8.
  • We honour the Global Privacy Platform (GPP) and Apple's Limit Ad Tracking setting.
  • Where regional law (e.g. EU ePrivacy) requires it, we present a CMP (Consent Management Platform) dialog aligned with the IAB TCF v2.2 specification.

14. Age Restrictions

Our Apps have the following age ratings across the major storefronts:

  • Apple App Store: 4+ (when the App contains no objectionable content), 9+, 12+ or 17+ as assigned by the App Review Board. The age rating is shown in the App listing and enforced by Apple.
  • Google Play: IARC age rating, typically Everyone / PEGI 3, but can be 7 / 12 / 16 / 18 depending on content.
  • Other stores: We apply equivalent ratings.

We do not knowingly collect or process Personal Data from children under the age of 13 (United States COPPA), under 16 (EU/UK GDPR unless lower with Member-State Consent), under 14 (China PIPL & Personal Information Protection Specification for Children), under 13 (Brazil LGPD for children), or under any higher age defined by the local law of your country.

Parents, teachers and guardians may request review or deletion of any data we may have inadvertently collected from a child by writing to contact@jianzhik.com. We will respond within 30 days.

17. GDPR (EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation (and its UK and Swiss equivalents) apply. You have the following rights:

  • Right of access — to obtain a copy of your Personal Data (Art. 15).
  • Right to rectification — to correct inaccurate data (Art. 16).
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — to have your data deleted (Art. 17).
  • Right to restriction of processing — to limit how we use your data (Art. 18).
  • Right to data portability — to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (Art. 20).
  • Right to object — to object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing (Art. 21).
  • Rights related to automated decision-making — not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing (Art. 22).
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. A directory is available from the European Data Protection Board.

Where we rely on consent for advertising personalisation (e.g. via the IAB TCF v2.2 CMP), you can revisit your decisions at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority:

  • EU: list at edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en
  • UK: ico.org.uk
  • Switzerland: edoeb.admin.ch

The legal bases we rely on are described in section 5. International transfers are described in section 24.

18. CCPA / CPRA (California, USA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), applies. You have the right to:

  • Know what categories of Personal Information we collect, the sources, the business purposes, and the categories of recipients.
  • Access the specific pieces of Personal Information collected about you in the preceding 12 months.
  • Delete Personal Information collected from you, subject to exceptions.
  • Correct inaccurate Personal Information.
  • Opt out of sale or sharing of your Personal Information. We do not "sell" Personal Information for money. Some advertising personalisation may constitute "sharing" under CPRA. You may opt out via the in-app "Privacy Choices" panel, via the platform-level toggles described in section 8, or by emailing contact@jianzhik.com.
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information — we do not collect sensitive personal information beyond what is strictly necessary for the Services.
  • Non-discrimination — we will not deny service, charge different prices or provide a different level of quality for exercising your rights.

19. PIPL (People's Republic of China)

If you are located in the People's Republic of China, the Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL), the Cybersecurity Law (CSL) and the Data Security Law (DSL) apply. Where our Apps are distributed via Huawei AppGallery, Xiaomi GetApps, OPPO Software Store, vivo App Store or any PRC channel, additional PIPL-specific rules apply:

  • We will only process your Personal Information with your separate, informed consent.
  • Cross-border transfers are subject to one of the lawful mechanisms (CAC security assessment, Standard Contract, Certification).
  • Sensitive Personal Information is processed only with a specific purpose, sufficient necessity and strict protection measures.
  • We honour the right to access, correct, delete, portability, withdraw consent and request explanation.

The PRC-specific local representative can be reached at contact@jianzhik.com. For Baidu, Tencent and other PRC-based ad networks, their respective privacy policies govern the data they process.

20. LGPD (Brazil)

If you are in Brazil, the Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados (LGPD) applies. You have rights of confirmation, access, correction, anonymisation, portability, deletion, information on sharing and information on the possibility of not providing consent. The Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) is the supervisory authority.

21. COPPA (United States - Children under 13)

We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from children under the age of 13 in a manner that requires verifiable parental consent under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Our Apps marked as suitable for children under 13 are reviewed for compliance and:

  • Disable behavioural advertising.
  • Use only contextual advertising (where supported).
  • Do not collect IDFA / GAID from child-directed builds.
  • Do not include third-party SDKs that target children.

Parents may request review or deletion of any data inadvertently collected by writing to contact@jianzhik.com. We will respond within 30 days.

22. PDPA (Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia)

For users in Singapore, Thailand and Malaysia, the Personal Data Protection Acts (PDPA) apply. We comply with the consent, notification, purpose, accuracy, protection, retention limitation, access & correction, and transfer limitation obligations.

23. Sharing & Disclosure

We share Personal Information only as described below:

  • Advertising & mediation partners as described in Section 9, after you grant consent where required.
  • Email & newsletter providers — for the field-notes newsletter, opt-in only.
  • Cloud infrastructure — encrypted hosting providers in the EEA, US, Asia for backup and analytics aggregation.
  • Customer support — first-party tools only. No third-party "session replay" tracking is used without your consent.
  • App store operators — anonymised install and crash data where required by the store policies.
  • Legal — when required by law, court order, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.
  • Corporate transactions — in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, with continued protection commitments.

We do not sell Personal Information for monetary consideration.

26. Security

We employ administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect Personal Information, including:

  • TLS 1.3 for all in-transit data.
  • AES-256 encryption for at-rest data.
  • Hardware-backed key storage (Apple Secure Enclave / Android StrongBox / TPM).
  • Annual third-party security review and penetration test.
  • Strict least-privilege access controls, multi-factor authentication, just-in-time access.
  • Encrypted backups, off-site replicated.
  • Incident response plan with 72-hour notification.

No system is 100% secure. If you believe your interaction with us is no longer secure, please notify us immediately at contact@jianzhik.com.

27. Your Rights & How to Exercise Them

Regardless of where you live, you have the following baseline rights. We will respond within 30 days (45 days under CCPA where extension is permitted).

  • Access — request a copy of Personal Data we hold about you.
  • Correction — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion — request deletion of Personal Data.
  • Portability — receive data in a portable format.
  • Opt-out of sale / sharing — opt out of advertising personalisation.
  • Restriction — limit how we use your data.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, without affecting prior processing.
  • Lodge a complaint — with your local supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@jianzhik.com with the subject "Privacy Request". For verification we may need to confirm your identity via a piece of information only you would know.

The Services are not directed at children under 13 (or higher where local law applies). We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from children. Where we publish Apps that may be used by children, we apply heightened protections and disable behavioural advertising.
