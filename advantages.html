// 02 - Eight Pillars

Eight structural advantages.

Each advantage is a design choice we made when we started, and refuse to compromise on today.

01

Local-first as default

Every product begins with a local-only assumption. Cloud sync is a privilege, never a requirement. We architect around the absence of network.

02

Generative depth

Twelve years of procedural audio, graphics and parameter system R&D. We do not bolt AI on at the end - it is the substrate of our craft.

03

Privacy by architecture

End-to-end encryption, hardware-backed keystores, zero-knowledge sync. Privacy is structural rather than cosmetic.

04

Refined restraint

We refuse the temptation to add. Every interaction must earn its weight in the interface - the rest is cut.

05

Deep specialisation

We are not a generalist agency. Our team has spent years in audio DSP, generative graphics, mobile UI motion and privacy engineering.

06

Small team, low overhead

No account managers, no PMs, no layers between you and the engineer who actually ships the code.

07

Transparent telemetry

Weekly demos, monthly retros, public changelog. You see what is being built - and why.

08

Long-term stewardship

Our products are still maintained five years later. We do not abandon what we ship. Sustainability is part of the work.

// 03 - Manifesto

Our working principles.

// jianzhik.principles() const principles = { "01_minimalist_design": "every pixel earns its place", "02_privacy_first": "structure, not stickers", "03_experience_above_all": "the user is the boss", "04_generative_depth": "algorithms over templates", "05_long_term_care": "we ship and we stay", "06_no_dark_patterns": "never. full stop." }; return principles.always;

A team that says no so the product can say yes.

We have learned, sometimes painfully, that the most respectful thing we can do for our users is to refuse them features they do not need. Privacy is not a marketing checkbox - it is the absence of unnecessary data collection.

Visual restraint is not a stylistic pose - it is the discipline of removing every element that does not serve comprehension or delight. The interface you do not see is the one that respects your attention.

Generative technology is not a buzzword - it is the careful application of algorithmic processes to create variety, surprise and personalisation that no template could ever deliver.

// 04 - Our Pipeline

Five stages. Zero theatre.

We optimise for honest progress. No status theatre, no invented rituals.

S / 01

Discover

Field studies, usage archaeology, the long quiet listening before any code is written.

S / 02

Prototype

Generative sketches, throwaway spikes, paper - fast mind tea. We test the wobble early.

S / 03

Build

Modular architecture, weekly release trains, full unit and UI tests. No cowboy merges.

S / 04

Harden

Privacy review, accessibility audit, App Store guideline scan, security pentest.

S / 05

Ship & Stay

Phased rollout, in-app feedback, post-launch iteration. The launch is the beginning.
